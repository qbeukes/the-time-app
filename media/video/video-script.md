## Production Notes

### Narration Style
- Male or female voice, warm and measured
- Not rushed — allow pauses between sentences
- Slightly reverberant/spacious mixing to match the cosmic visuals
- Approximate word count: ~310 words (~2:10 of narration, leaving room for visual breathing)

### Music Direction
- Ambient, cosmic, slowly building
- Start minimal (piano/pad), build through mid-section, resolve gently
- No lyrics
- Suggested search terms: "cinematic ambient space" / "cosmic documentary score"
- Royalty-free sources: Epidemic Sound, Artlist, or YouTube Audio Library

### Visual Direction for AI Video Generation
- All scenes should use slow, deliberate camera movement (Ken Burns style)
- Predominant palette: deep navy/black backgrounds, warm gold/copper accents
- The app screenshots should appear within a clean phone mockup frame
- Scene transitions: dissolves (2 second crossfades)
- Text overlays: clean sans-serif font (Inter or similar), semi-transparent background panel
- Maintain 16:9 aspect ratio throughout
