Privacy Policy

Effective date: 27 August 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to The Time App, published by Quintin Beukes (also Quintin Beukes Technologies).

Information We Collect

The Time App is designed to operate offline.

The app does not collect, transmit, store, or share personal information with us or with any cloud service operated by us.

Any information entered into or generated by the app is stored locally on the user's device and remains under the user's control.

Internet and Cloud Services

The app does not require an online account and does not upload application data to our servers.

We do not operate a cloud service for storing user information associated with this app.

Analytics and Advertising

The app does not use analytics services, advertising services, or tracking technologies to collect information about users.

The app does not sell or share personal information with third parties.

Device Permissions

The app may request device permissions where required for its normal functionality.

These permissions are used only to provide the relevant feature on the user's device.

Information accessed through these permissions is not transmitted to us unless explicitly stated within the app.

Data Retention and Deletion

Because we do not collect or store user data on our servers, we do not retain personal information associated with the app.

Users can remove locally stored application data by clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app.

Children's Privacy

The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the application's functionality or data-handling practices change.

Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised effective date.
